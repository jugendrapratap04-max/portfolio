Put your static assets here. The config references these by default:

  profile.jpg   -> your photo (square works best, e.g. 600x600)
  resume.pdf    -> your downloadable CV (Hero "Resume" button)
  og-image.png  -> social share image, 1200x630 (link previews)
  favicon.ico   -> browser tab icon

Until you add profile.jpg, the hero shows your initial on a gradient — no broken image.
Project & certificate images can be local (place here and use "/myimage.png")
or remote URLs (add the host to next.config.mjs -> images.remotePatterns).
